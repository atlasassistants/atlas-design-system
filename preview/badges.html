Rules

  • Yellow Green on light is a fill with Deep Blue text (17.1:1). Never Yellow Green text, icons, or outlines on light.
  • Neutral tags use --accent-bg with --accent-text; they flip to Yellow Green on dark.
  • Sentence case inside badges. The eyebrow badge is the only uppercase badge.
  • No decorative status dots. Add an icon only when it carries meaning.
