Patterns

Four brand textures from the guidelines and the designer's assets: the symbol watermark, the watermark outlines, the six-tab color strip, and the corner wash. Keep them quiet: at most two textures on one object. On gradients, the watermark outlines may pair with the symbol watermark. On light, the watermark may pair with the corner wash. Brand covers pair the strip with the corner wash, as on guidelines page 1.

Symbol watermark

The symbol file, very large and cropped by the edge (guidelines pages 7 and 8). White symbol at 6 to 10% on gradients; gradient symbol at 4 to 6% on light.

Rules

  • The watermark outlines and the white symbol watermark belong on deep, vivid, or brand gradients. Never on Pearl White.
  • A subtle paper texture on light is optional and stays below 4% visible.
  • Retired with the 2026 cosmic system: its dark backdrops, cosmic orbital rings, and glow halos.
