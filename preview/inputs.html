Rules

  • Label above, helper or error below. Never use the placeholder as the label.
  • Placeholder text uses --fg-3 (5.3:1 on white). Disabled text may drop to --fg-4.
  • Errors use a 2px --fg-1 border, an alert icon, and a plain sentence that says how to fix it. The palette has no red; do not add one.
