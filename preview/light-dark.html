Light and dark

Light is the default: set nothing, :root is light. Dark is opt-in with data-theme="dark" on <html>, <body>, or any section. The same components render both; only the tokens change.

One component set, two modes

Mixing

A light page may hold dark regions; a dark page may hold light regions. Scope each region with its own data-theme. Keep the restraint rule: at most one dark object and one gradient band per screen.

Rules

  • Switch the logo file with the surface: atlas-logo-dark.png on light, atlas-logo-white.png on Deep Blue.
  • One primary action color per surface: Vivid Purple on light, Yellow Green on dark.
  • A dark footer is allowed as a page close; it counts as the screen's dark object.
