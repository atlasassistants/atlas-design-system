Color

Six colors from the brand guidelines (page 6), each with four tints. Light is the default: Pearl White pages, Deep Blue text, Vivid Purple actions. Yellow Green is a fill on light and the accent on dark.

Rules

  • Yellow Green on light surfaces is a fill only: a highlighter mark, a badge, or a button with Deep Blue text.
  • One primary action color per surface: Vivid Purple on light, Yellow Green on dark.
  • No new hues. Softer fills, tags, and chart series use the 80/60/40/20 tints.
  • Gradients come from three families: brand, deep, and vivid (see gradients).
  • Light pages show at most one dark or gradient object and one gradient band per screen.
