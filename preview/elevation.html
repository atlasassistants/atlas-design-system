Elevation

On light, shadows are soft and tinted with Deep Blue. On dark, depth comes from lighter surfaces and an inner top highlight. No glow halos on light.

Light (default)

--shadow-1Resting surfaces, inputs
--shadow-2Card hover, menus
--shadow-3Primary button
--shadow-4Hero object only
--shadow-glowFeatured card

Dark

--shadow-1Inner top highlight
--shadow-2Lifted surface
--shadow-3Primary button
--shadow-4Hero object
--shadow-glowFeatured card

Rules

  • Elevation shows hierarchy. If nothing is above anything else, use a hairline instead.
  • One --shadow-4 per screen: the hero object.
  • No pure black shadows on light and no colored glow halos.
