Gradients

Three families, all observed in the guidelines. Gradients are objects and bands, not page backgrounds. On a light page, show at most one gradient object and one gradient band per screen; this spec page breaks that rule on purpose.

The logo gradient

Brand

--gradient-brand · 135°
Soft Blue Violet 0%Electric Purple 100%

The symbol's own gradient. Use it on the symbol, avatars, and small accent fills. Text on it is Deep Blue (4.8:1 or better), as on guidelines page 8.

Hero objects, feature bands, deck covers, social

Deep

--gradient-deep · 135°
Electric Purple 0%Vivid Purple 42%Deep Blue 100%

Guidelines pages 2 and 5. White headlines, Yellow Green accents. Keep small text and Yellow Green off the Electric Purple corner (Yellow Green there is 2.7:1); place them over the Vivid Purple and Deep Blue stretch.

Vivid

--gradient-vivid · 160°
Vivid Purple 0%Deep Blue 100%

Guidelines page 8. The highest-contrast family: Yellow Green holds 7.1:1 on Vivid Purple and 17.1:1 on Deep Blue.

On light surfaces

Two light-only treatments. Neither counts as a gradient object.

Corner wash on Pearl White
--wash-corner: an Electric Purple bloom at 22% in one corner of a hero or section (guidelines page 1).
Your zone of genius
.grad-text on light: Vivid Purple to Electric Purple, large display text only. On dark the accent phrase is solid Yellow Green.

Rules

  • No other gradients. No mesh blobs, glows, or gradients behind body text on light.
  • Gradient containers carry data-theme="dark" so buttons and accents switch to Yellow Green.
  • The watermark outlines and the symbol watermark may sit on deep and vivid gradients (see patterns).
