Buttons

Pill buttons in Poppins Semi-Bold, 48px tall. The primary action is Vivid Purple with white text on light, and Yellow Green with Deep Blue text on dark. Arrows are SVG icons, never unicode characters.

Variants on light and dark

Same markup in both panels. The dark panel only adds data-theme="dark"; the tokens switch the colors.
